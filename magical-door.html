Privacy Policy - Mimooza's Magical Door

Effective date: October 25, 2026

Developer: Mimooza Kids (the Mimooza Kids YouTube channel)

App: Mimooza's Magical Door (Android package com.mimoozakids.magicaldoor)

Mimooza's Magical Door is a learning game for children ages 2 to 8. We designed it so that no personal information is collected, stored outside the device, or shared with anyone. This policy explains exactly what the app does.

What the app does not do

  • It does not collect, store or send personal information of any kind: no names, e-mail addresses, photos, contacts, location or device identifiers.
  • It does not contain advertising or any advertising SDK.
  • It does not contain analytics, tracking or crash-reporting services.
  • It does not require an account or a sign-in.
  • It does not connect to the internet. The app has no internet permission; every picture, sound and voice line is included in the app itself.
  • It has no chat, no messages and no way for children to share content with other people.
  • It has no links to websites, social networks or other apps.

Information stored on the device only

The app remembers the child's progress (stars, stickers, the Rainbow Jar, the garden) and the grown-ups' settings (voice, music, microphone) in the app's own storage on the device. This information never leaves the device, except through Android's normal app backup to the device owner's own Google account, which the device owner controls in Android settings. Uninstalling the app deletes it.

Microphone

Two games ("Blow out the candle" and "Blow the clouds away") can use the microphone to detect blowing. The microphone is off by default. A grown-up has to turn it on behind a parent gate (a multiplication question a young child cannot answer), and can turn it off again at any time in the grown-ups settings. When it is on, the app only measures the loudness of the sound, on the device, while that game is open. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere. Without the microphone, the child can play the same games by tapping and holding a button.

Motion sensor

"Rock Wimbi to sleep", "Shake for snow" and the Rainbow Jar use the device's motion sensor to notice rocking, shaking and tilting. The readings are used on the device at that moment and are not stored or sent.

"Save as picture"

The "Story of my day" screen can create a picture of the child's day. When the child or a grown-up taps "Save as picture", the Android share sheet opens and the grown-up chooses where the picture goes (for example the phone's photos). The app itself does not send the picture anywhere.

Purchases

Doors 1 and 2 are free. Doors 3, 4 and 5 can be unlocked once with a one-time purchase that is handled entirely by Google Play. The purchase button is behind the parent gate. We do not receive any payment details; Google Play handles the payment and tells the app only whether the purchase was made. There are no subscriptions and no in-game currency.

Children's privacy (COPPA)

The app is designed for children and complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and the Google Play Families policy by not collecting personal information from anyone.

Changes

If the app ever changes in a way that affects this policy, the policy will be updated here and the effective date changed.
