MIMOOZA ABC: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 5, 2026

The short version

MIMOOZA ABC (“the app”) is an alphabet and early reading app for children. We do not collect, receive, store or share any personal information from children or from parents.

The app has no accounts, no sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party software kits that collect data. It does not connect to the internet to play, and it does not send anything to us or to anyone else.

Who we are

The app is made by Mimooza Kids Learning (“we”, “us”). You can reach us at mimoozakids@gmail.com or by mail at 21 Shabazi Street, Apartment 10, 5th Floor, Rehovot, Israel.

What stays on your device

To remember where your child is, the app saves progress on the device itself: stars, finished levels, how many answers were right, time spent learning, the settings a grown-up chose, and for each child the character picture they picked and, if a grown-up typed one, a first name or nickname, and the child’s age (2 to 10), used only to pick the level. A name is optional.

This information stays in the app’s own storage on the device. We never receive it and cannot see it.

You can erase it at any time: use “Reset progress” or remove a child in the Parent Center, or uninstall the app.

Children’s names

On the “Who is playing?” screen (a grown-up holds the + or the pencil for a second) a grown-up gives each child an age and may give a first name or nickname (a name can also be given in the Parent Center), so brothers and sisters can find their own picture and parents can see each child’s report. The name is optional. The name and the age are saved only on the device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. You can change or delete them at any time.

A grown-up may also type a first name to print on a certificate or an achievement picture. That name is used only to draw the picture on the device and is not saved.

Sharing and the backup code

Only a grown-up can share, from the Parent Center, which is behind a grown-up math question. Sharing opens the device’s own share menu, so you choose where a picture goes. The app itself sends nothing. Once you share a picture, the service you choose handles it under its own privacy policy.

The backup code (for moving progress to a new device) holds only the progress and the children’s first names and ages described above. It leaves the device only if a grown-up copies it.

Device features

In the “Shake & Pour” activity the app reads the device’s motion sensor on the device, only to notice a shake. The app may also use a short vibration as gentle feedback.

The microphone is off unless a grown-up turns it on in the Parent Center. Only one game can use it then: in “Magic Voice” your child records one word and hears it played back in funny voices: the recording stays only in the device’s memory while the game is open, and it is never saved, never sent to us or anyone else, and is gone when your child moves on. The “Talking Forest” is played by tapping only.

The app does not use the camera, location or contacts, and it never looks at your photos. If a grown-up chooses “Save Image” when sharing an achievement picture, the device adds that one picture to Photos.

Purchases

The family plan (a monthly or yearly subscription) can be bought only in the Parent Center, behind the grown-up question. Payment is handled by the app store (Google Play or the Apple App Store). We do not receive payment card details. The store may give us limited order information, such as an order number, that we need for refunds and support.

You can cancel the subscription at any time in the app store’s subscription settings. The app only checks with the store, on the device, whether the plan is active.

The app store and your device maker may collect information under their own privacy policies. We do not control that.

Children’s privacy (COPPA)

The app is made for children, and we follow the U.S. Children’s Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). Because we do not collect personal information from children, there is nothing that needs parental consent.

If this ever changes, we will update this policy first and we will ask for verifiable parental consent before collecting anything.

Your choices as a parent

Because we hold no information about you or your child, there is nothing on our side to look at, correct or delete. Everything the app remembers is on your device, and you can erase it as described above.

If you write to us, we use your email only to answer you, we do not share it, and we delete the conversation within 90 days after your question is resolved. You can also ask us to delete it sooner by writing to mimoozakids@gmail.com.

Security

Progress is kept in the app’s private storage, protected by your device’s own security. Nothing is sent over the internet, so nothing can be intercepted on the way.

Links

The app has no ads and no links to websites or social media in the parts children use.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new date. If a change affects what the app does with information, we will say so in the app update notes as well.
