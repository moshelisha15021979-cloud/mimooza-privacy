Mimooza World – Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2026

Mimooza World ("the app") is made by Mimooza Kids for children ages 2–8 and their parents. We take children's privacy seriously and designed the app to collect no personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not ask for, collect, store or share any personal information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no chat and no location access.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not use the advertising ID.

Data stored on the device

The app saves small game settings (such as music on/off and whether the full version is unlocked) only on the device itself. This data never leaves the device and is removed when the app is uninstalled.

Purchases

The optional one-time "unlock everything" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play. We never see or store payment details. Purchases are protected by a parent gate.

Links outside the app

The only external link (to our YouTube channel) is inside the grown-ups area, behind a parent gate.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, we comply with COPPA and Google Play's Families Policy. If you believe information was collected by mistake, contact us and we will delete it.
