Mimooza World – Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Mimooza World ("the app") is made by Mimooza Kids for children ages 2–10 and their parents. We take children's privacy seriously and designed the app to collect no personal information.

Information we collect

None. The app does not collect or share any personal information from children or adults. There are no accounts, no sign-ups, no chat, no camera and no location access. On the "Who is playing?" screen a grown-up taps each child's age and may type a first name (the name is optional), so brothers and sisters each keep their own progress; the name and age stay only on the device (unless a grown-up copies a backup code, see below) and are never sent to us or anyone else.

Advertising and tracking

The app contains no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools. It does not use the advertising ID.

Data stored on the device

The app saves small game settings (such as voice on/off, each child's game stars, the child's own drawings, the first name, age and picture chosen on "Who is playing?" and whether the full version is unlocked) only on the device itself. The app never sends this data anywhere, and it is removed when the app is uninstalled. A grown-up can remove a child and their progress at any time in the grown-ups corner. A grown-up can also make a backup code in the grown-ups corner to move the children to a new device: it holds only each child's game progress (with their squishies; drawings stay on the old device) and the children's first names, ages and pictures, nothing else, and it is not sent anywhere: it leaves the device only if a grown-up copies it. The one-time purchase is not in the code ("Restore purchase" brings it back).

Purchases

The optional one-time "unlock everything" purchase is handled entirely by Google Play (Android) or the Apple App Store (iPhone/iPad). We never see or store payment details. Purchases are protected by a parent gate.

Links outside the app

The app has no links that leave the app.

Children's privacy

Because we collect no personal information, we comply with COPPA, Google Play's Families Policy and the App Store's rules for apps in the Kids category. If you believe information was collected by mistake, contact us and we will delete it.
